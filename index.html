03 / A repeatable method

Your Kakobuy shopping guide, one check at a time.

A shorter list with useful notes is easier to compare than a long list of unexplained links.

Keep a record of the exact variant and the date you checked it. Prices and availability can change.

  1. Pick a category and a purpose

    Decide what you need: an everyday sneaker, an extra layer or a small accessory. Choose a budget before browsing.

  2. Record the exact product details

    Save the listing link, color, size, stated fabric or material, current price and any unanswered questions.

  3. Compare measurements and evidence

    Use the item’s size chart and available photos. Seller images show presentation; they do not independently establish quality.

  4. Estimate the full cost before deciding

    Include applicable fees, shipping and taxes or duties. Confirm payment, return and shipping terms with the relevant service.

What is a Kakobuy spreadsheet?

A Kakobuy spreadsheet is a product research list used to organize shopping links and compare items. Depending on the creator, it may include categories, prices, sizing notes and photos. Details should be verified on the destination listing.

Does this page contain a downloadable spreadsheet?

No. This page is a category browsing guide and shopping checklist. Its links lead to CAFANS rather than a downloadable spreadsheet.

Is this the official Kakobuy website?

No. This is an independent informational page linking to CAFANS. It is not affiliated with, endorsed by or operated by Kakobuy.

What should I check before choosing a product?

Check the exact variant, measurements, materials, listing photos, current price and available shipping information. Keep unconfirmed details marked as unknown.

Can a spreadsheet guarantee product quality or delivery?

No. A spreadsheet organizes information but cannot guarantee authenticity, quality, availability, seller performance or delivery. Verify current details and applicable policies before paying.

How should I compare the total cost of two items?

Use the same currency and include the item price, applicable service fees, domestic shipping, international shipping and any applicable taxes or duties. Treat the result as an estimate until the relevant charges are confirmed.
